01

What we found

Captify sells a premium, high consideration product to an audience that researches slowly and often asks family before buying. The ads have to reach two different buyers, earn trust before asking for the sale, and report revenue back to the ad platforms.

High impact

One product, two buyers, two different messages

Your reviews show the pattern clearly. Many buyers are wives, sons and cousins buying for someone with hearing loss. One generic ad set cannot speak to both groups. The user wants to be part of the conversation again. The family buyer wants to know it works, that it is easy to set up and that returns are simple.

Ad spend has to optimize toward purchases, not clicks

With a premium price and a long research window, the ad platforms only learn who will buy if they receive the purchase event with its order value from Shopify. Without browser and server side signals, Meta and Google see too few sales to optimize properly.

The 30 day plan

Tracking comes first, then search demand, then Meta prospecting for both buyers. Retargeting and budget shifts come last, once the data is reliable.

Week 1Week 2Week 3Week 4
Tracking and audit
Google Search and Shopping
Meta, users and families

Weekly reporting
1

Week 1: Audit and rebuild purchase tracking

  • Shopify purchases with order value into GA4, Google Ads enhanced conversions and Meta Conversions API, with deduplication
  • Secondary signals: add to cart, lens selection, insurance page views
  • Review current campaigns, search terms and audiences, and cut obvious waste

Reason: every later decision depends on the platforms seeing real sales and their value.

2

Week 2: Build Google Search and Shopping around intent

  • Search campaigns by cluster: category, problem, gift, payment and brand
  • Shopping feed for Pro and Myvu with clean titles, prices and review ratings
  • Exclude terms for cheap smart glasses and general AR glasses

Reason: people already searching for a solution are the cheapest sales to win.

3

Week 3: Launch Meta for two buyers

  • Prospecting for users 60+ and for family buyers 35 to 60, with separate creative
  • Captioned video built from real customer reviews and restaurant or family dinner moments
  • Facebook placements first, with larger text and simple calls to action

Reason: Meta creates demand from people who do not yet know captioning glasses exist.

4

Week 4: Retarget visitors with trust messages and move budget to winners

  • Retargeting in sequence: reviews, then the 14 day trial, then FSA, HSA and insurance
  • Shift budget toward the ad sets and keywords with the lowest cost per purchase
  • Set a target cost per purchase and ROAS for month two

Reason: older buyers rarely purchase on the first visit, so the follow up has to answer their doubts.

5

Every week: A plain English report

  • Spend, purchases, cost per purchase and ROAS by channel and audience
  • What changed, why it changed, and what happens next week

Reason: you see exactly where each dollar went and what it returned.
